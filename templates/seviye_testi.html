My Best Friend

My name is Elif. I am twelve years old and I live in Bayrampaşa, İstanbul. I have a best friend. Her name is Zeynep. We are in the same class at school. Zeynep is tall and has long black hair. She loves reading books and playing the piano.

Every Saturday, we go to the park near our house. We ride our bikes and eat sandwiches that my mother prepares for us. Sometimes we play football with our friends. In winter, we cannot go to the park, so we stay at home and watch cartoons.

Zeynep's favourite subject is English. She wants to be an English teacher when she grows up. I want to be a doctor. We are different, but we are very good friends.
